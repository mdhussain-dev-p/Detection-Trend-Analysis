TOPIC DETECTION AND TREND ANALYSIS
YEARLY RESEARCH TRENDS — 2018 TO 2024
=====================================

Final model: NMF, K=10, trained on the full filtered corpus.
Topic numbers refer to the full-corpus model.

Two annual measures are calculated:
1. Dominant-topic share: percentage of papers assigned primarily
   to a topic in a particular year.
2. Mean topic activation share: average normalised NMF activation
   for each topic across modelled papers in that year.

Both measures are calculated using modelled papers as the topic
denominator. Unassigned papers remain in annual corpus counts but
are excluded from topic-share calculations.

The mean activation shares sum to 100 percent for each year.
NMF activations are not calibrated probabilities.

This is retrospective descriptive analysis, not causal inference
or forecasting.
